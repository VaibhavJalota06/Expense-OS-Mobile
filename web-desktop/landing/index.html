Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Expense OS really free?

Yes, 100% free. There are no trial periods, no locked features, and no credit card required. It is built as a clean, practical tool for everyone.

How does syncing between my phone and computer work?

Your data is saved securely to your account using Supabase cloud database. Whenever you open the app on your computer or phone, everything stays in sync. If you lose internet connection, you can keep adding expenses normally and they will sync up when you reconnect.

Can I export or backup my data anytime?

Yes. You can download all your transaction history and budgets anytime as a standard CSV spreadsheet or a JSON backup file with one click.
