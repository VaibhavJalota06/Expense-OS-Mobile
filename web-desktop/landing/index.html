Frequently Asked Questions

Is Expense OS free to use?

Yes, 100%! Expense OS is completely free with zero hidden subscriptions, microtransactions, or locked features.

How does cross-app syncing work?

Every transaction is dual-written to local device storage and live Supabase Postgres tables. If you are offline, changes are cached locally and synchronized the moment you reconnect.

Can I export my data anytime?

Yes! You can export your full transaction history and ledgers into standard CSV spreadsheets or backup JSON format with a single click.
