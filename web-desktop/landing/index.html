Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are clear answers to the most common questions about Expense OS.

Is Expense OS completely free to use?

Yes, 100%! Expense OS is completely free to use with zero hidden fees or subscription lockouts.

How do I import my bank statement?

Simply click "Import Bank CSV" or drag & drop your statement file (PDF, Excel .xlsx/.xls, or CSV). Expense OS automatically detects transaction dates, narration descriptions, and amounts.

Is my financial data safe and private?

Absolutely. Your financial records are stored securely on your own device and local session storage. We never sell or share your data.

Can I use Expense OS without internet?

Yes! Expense OS has 100% offline support. You can add expenses, edit transactions, and view charts anytime offline.
